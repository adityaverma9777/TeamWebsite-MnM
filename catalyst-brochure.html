// What is Catalyst?

Build with AI.
Ship in Hours.

Catalyst is a high-energy, solo-participation coding hackathon designed to test your ability to build with AI. Brought to you by MnM in collaboration with PromptWars.

Participants receive a surprise problem statement on the day of the event. The challenge? Use AI tools and sharp prompting to build a working solution in just a few intense hours.

"No teams. No templates.
Just you, a prompt, and the clock."

// What is PromptWars?

Prompting is
the New Coding.

PromptWars is a challenge for anyone who loves building — developers, designers, professionals, or anyone with big ideas and a sharp instinct for prompting.

Participants showcase their problem-solving skills through prompt-based development, creating real solutions using AI. It's where creativity meets engineering at speed.

PromptWars

A global prompt-based challenge platform where builders create solutions entirely through AI-assisted workflows.

// AI Engine

Google Antigravity

The official AI coding IDE. All participants build using Antigravity's agent-assisted development workflow.

Why Solo?

Solo participation ensures authentic skill assessment. Every line of code, every architectural decision is yours alone — making evaluation transparent and fair.

Campus

We are looking to collaborate with universities, campus communities, and student tech groups to bring Catalyst to ambitious student builders. This is a purely non-monetary partnership focused on student growth and community building.

Our primary aim is to give students exposure to AI, open-source projects, and practical ways to contribute to real-world tech projects.

Long-Term Vision

We want to work with campus communities beyond this event to create more open-source programmes, workshops, and student-led initiatives globally.
