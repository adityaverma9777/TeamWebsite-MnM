The Minds Behind Makers Need More.

Makers Need More was founded by Aditya Verma and Manika Kutiyal to bridge the gap between student talent, open source, and production engineering — helping developers learn by shipping and businesses grow through collaboration.

Building the Highway We Wished Existed.

Starting out in software development is often full of friction, fragmented tutorials, and isolation. We started MnM to build an ecosystem where students can learn by doing, compete in hackathons, contribute to real production codebases, and establish a verifiable track record of shipped software.

College should be more than exams and lectures. By channeling student drive into open-source tooling, hands-on mentorship, and free digital support for emerging local businesses, we empower makers to graduate with true engineering readiness.
