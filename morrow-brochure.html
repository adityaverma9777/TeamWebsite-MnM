// What is Morrow?

Built to Last
Beyond the Weekend

Morrow is an open-source hackathon organised by MnM (Makers Need More) where participants build solutions that continue to benefit people even after the event ends.

Every project is encouraged to remain open source so others can learn from it, contribute to it, and improve it over time.

"Because the best software shouldn't disappear after judging."

// Why Open Source?

Impact Without
an Expiry Date

Every line of code has the potential to help someone else. Whether you're fixing a small issue or building the next great platform, your contribution becomes part of something bigger than a weekend hackathon.

At Morrow, we believe technology built in the open is technology that grows. Your project doesn't end at the submission deadline — it begins there. By open-sourcing your work, you invite collaboration, learning, and real-world impact.

One motive. Thousands of builders. Infinite possibilities. mnmworks.xyz
