Empowering Students Globally Without Barriers.

Makers Need More (MnM) is an open student community where anyone can learn by building real things. We believe true engineering skills don't come from memorizing textbooks or watching endless tutorials - they come from getting your hands dirty with real code, collaborating with passionate peers, and shipping software that real people use. Everything we offer is 100% free for students with zero paywalls and no hidden costs. From hackathons and workshops to contributing to open-source software, we give you the guidance, tools, and platform to build a real portfolio. We also give back to society by building free websites and digital tools for underprivileged small businesses.

End-to-End Engineering & Digital Services.

Our student teams design, engineer, and ship production software across the modern tech stack.

Frontend Development

Fast, responsive, and accessible user interfaces built with React, Next.js, and Tailwind CSS. We make sure every screen loads instantly and feels great to interact with.

Backend Engineering

Solid server systems, clean REST and GraphQL APIs, and reliable databases powered by Node.js, Python, FastAPI, and PostgreSQL. Built for reliability and heavy traffic.

AI & Intelligent Systems

Practical machine learning pipelines, model deployment, and automation. We integrate artificial intelligence into real tools that solve everyday operational bottlenecks.

Full-Stack Digital Products

Complete digital applications from concept to live deployment. Includes secure user authentication, database management, payment processing, and cloud hosting.

Free Digital Infrastructure for Small Businesses

We believe tech should build bridges, not barriers. We design, build, and deploy free modern websites and cloud infrastructure for underprivileged small businesses, helping local founders establish their digital presence at zero cost.

Built on Core Values.

Three founding principles guide every project, competition, and event we organize.

01

Privacy First

Your data belongs to you. We build safe, secure, and zero-tracking spaces where student work, identity, and ideas are strictly respected.

02

100% Free Access

No paywalls, no subscriptions, and zero hidden charges. Practical learning and mentorship should always be open to every motivated student.

03

Learn by Doing

We foster a supportive environment where experimentation is celebrated, mistakes are seen as progress, and real skills are built by shipping.

Built by Students, for Students.

A self-governing community created to replace theory-heavy education with hands-on development and social impact.

A Clear Path into Real Tech

Starting out in software development is confusing and full of hurdles. We created MnM to give students a direct path: participate in hackathons, work on open-source codebases, collaborate with mentors, and graduate with a verifiable public record of shipped software.

Solving Real Community Problems

College should mean far more than just exams and lectures. We channel student talent into building free websites and tools for small local businesses, ensuring our members learn empathy, teamwork, and practical problem solving alongside technical excellence.

Verified Career Credentials

Interns gain hands-on production experience, custom email accounts for LinkedIn verification, physical completion certificates, and direct referral opportunities - turning community contribution into career acceleration.
