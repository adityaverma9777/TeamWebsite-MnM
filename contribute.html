StreetIQ

The AI-Powered Road Intelligence & Navigation Platform

StreetIQ is a next-generation navigation platform that tells you what is actually on the road. It provides real-time turn-by-turn navigation overlaid with live crowdsourced hazard data potholes, waterlogging, cracks, and debris detected entirely on-device using a custom-trained YOLOv8 model running in the browser at 15fps via TensorFlow.js. Video feeds are never sent to a server. Only GPS coordinates and hazard metadata are transmitted, preserving absolute privacy.

Custom-Trained YOLOv8 Model

Hand-trained on thousands of real Indian road images to detect cracks, potholes, waterlogging, and debris across varied lighting and traffic conditions.

On-Device AI — Zero Video Uploads

All inference runs locally in your browser. Nothing leaves your device except GPS coordinates when a hazard is confirmed.

Real-Time Hazard Broadcasting

The moment a hazard is detected, WebSockets instantly broadcast it to every other driver in the area via the Supabase Realtime backend.

Full Navigation Stack

Smooth turn-by-turn navigation powered by OSRM with custom traffic multipliers and Photon geocoding without third-party map API reliance.

Tech Stack

React + Vite TensorFlow.js YOLOv8 Node.js + TypeScript OSRM Supabase WebSockets React-Leaflet

Kavach

Digital Safety & Scam Protection for Senior Citizens · वरिष्ठ नागरिकों के लिए डिजिटल सुरक्षा

Kavach ("shield" in Hindi) is a privacy-first Android app safeguarding senior citizens from digital financial fraud — OTP scams, fake KYC / account-blocked SMS, extortion threats, lottery baits, and malicious APKs distributed via WhatsApp. Operating 100% offline with zero telemetry, Kavach scores threats locally, reads out warnings aloud in plain English or Hindi using on-device text-to-speech, and instantly alerts a trusted family member when high-risk activity is detected.

Automatic SMS Interception

On-device BroadcastReceiver scans incoming SMS in real time, detecting phishing links, banking extortion, urgency patterns, and OTP solicitations with zero cloud transmission.

WhatsApp & Clipboard Scanner

One-tap verification for suspicious forwarded WhatsApp and Telegram messages with bilingual risk assessment (LOW, SUSPICIOUS, HIGH) and audio readout in English and Hindi.

Pre-Install APK Analysis

Deep static manifest analysis inspecting dangerous permissions (READ_SMS, accessibility services, device admin) to flag and block banking trojans before installation.

Trusted Contact & Emergency SOS

Dispatches privacy-safe SMS alerts to a designated family member on high-risk detections, with one-tap dialing for National Cybercrime Helpline 1930.

Tech Stack

Android SDK (minSdk 26) Kotlin WebView Bridge BroadcastReceiver PackageManager Static Analysis TextToSpeech (en-IN / hi-IN) SQLite Threat DB Firebase Phone Auth

Operating with a zero-install architecture that runs entirely in the browser, ANTAR evaluates trajectory deviations, unexpected checkpoint delays, and road segment congestion to trigger predictive assistance before an incident escalates into a crisis.

In-Browser Spatial Intelligence

Offline-Ready Architecture

Zero cloud dependency for local simulation, checkpoint verification, and offline topological route graph parsing.

Deterministic Delay Scoring

Calculates probabilistic arrival windows based on road terrain, historical transit averages, and environmental factors.

Interactive Corridor Visualizer

CargoProof

Pay for Evidence. Verify the Claim. Settle with Proof.

CargoProof bridges real-world logistics with decentralized finance by enabling autonomous AI agents to purchase independent multi-modal evidence per shipment via x402 micropayments on the Algorand blockchain. A deterministic rule-based verification engine processes sensor telemetry, carrier bills of lading, and damage reports to execute automated escrow releases, holds, or rejections with cryptographic audit trails.

Algorand x402 Micropayments

Procures authenticated third-party sensor telemetry and surveyor reports on-chain using micro-transactions with instant finality.

Deterministic Rule Engine

Eliminates subjective arbitration by executing rule-based tri-state settlement: VERIFIED Release, HOLD Review, or REJECT.

Autonomous Agent Architecture

AI agents evaluate damage claims, cross-verify carrier GPS logs, and settle escrow balances without human bottlenecking.

Cryptographic Audit Ledger

Generates tamper-proof proofs of verification for insurance underwriters, freight forwarders, and logistics operators.

Tech Stack

Algorand SDK x402 Protocol FastAPI Node.js TypeScript Python SQLite

ResQNet

AI Emergency Response & Coordination Intelligence Grid

ResQNet transforms emergency trauma dispatch by integrating autonomous kinematic crash sensing on mobile devices with a high-throughput Bayesian fusion backend. ResQNet calculates a 0–100 polytrauma injury severity score within seconds of impact, automatically alerts the nearest trauma centers with zero-minute pre-alerts, and dynamically routes ambulances using live OSRM road graph topology.

Autonomous Crash Sensing

On-device accelerometer and gyroscope heuristics detect collision impact profiles with high accuracy.

Bayesian Multi-Source Fusion

Fuses impact telemetry, caller acoustic stress analysis, and camera feeds to eliminate false alarms and prioritize dispatch.

0–100 Polytrauma Injury Triage

Predicts clinical injury severity to recommend Level 1, 2, or 3 trauma center dispatch and specialized surgery preparation.

Zero-Minute Hospital Pre-Alerts

Transmits patient vitals, ETA, and emergency blood transfusion requirements to emergency room teams while in transit.

Honeypot Orchestrator

Honeypot Orchestrator is a modular cyber deception platform engineered for security operations teams and researchers. By spinning up lightweight, realistic decoy services (SSH, FTP, HTTP, Database), the system intercepts malicious probes, parses attacker behavioral patterns, trains machine learning classifiers on zero-day payloads, and correlates attacker kill-chains on a live interactive command dashboard.

Multi-Service Decoy Emulation

Configurable decoy listeners simulating vulnerable enterprise servers to attract adversaries and isolate malicious actors.

ML Attack-Chain Classification

Categorizes automated scanning bots, credential stuffers, brute-force attacks, and targeted exploits in real time.

Real-Time Kill-Chain Visualization

Tracks attacker lateral movement, command execution history, and dropped payloads through an interactive dashboard.

Dynamic Threat Scoring

Scores origin IP addresses, ASN reputation, and vulnerability probe intensities to automate firewall rule synthesis.

Emergency E-Card

Emergency E-Card is an open-source emergency coordination platform connecting individuals in distress with both emergency services and vetted nearby citizen responders. By pairing a secure, QR-accessible digital medical card (blood type, allergies, emergency contacts) with instantaneous WebSockets geolocation broadcasting, Emergency E-Card bridges critical golden-hour response gaps.

Instant One-Tap SOS Broadcast

Pushes live GPS coordinates and medical vitals to designated emergency contacts and nearby responders simultaneously.

Scannable Medical Emergency Profile

Instant lockscreen / wallet QR code allowing first responders to view critical health data without unlocking the device.

Hyperlocal Bystander Network

Alerts registered first-aid trained civilians within 500m for rapid CPR and immediate assistance before ambulances arrive.

Real-Time Dispatch Tracking

Parakh (परख)

High-Performance Deepfake Face Detection & Synthetic Media Verification

Parakh (Hindi for "scrutiny/test") is a specialized computer vision and deep learning system built to combat synthetic media threats, identity fraud, and AI-generated disinformation. Benchmark-trained against industry-standard datasets like FaceForensics++, Parakh detects subtle frame blending artifacts, temporal eye-blink inconsistencies, and frequency-domain anomalies across image and video uploads.

Binary Deepfake Classifier

Deep convolutional & transformer network trained on thousands of authentic and manipulated faces for high-precision inference.

Spatial Attention Heatmaps

Highlights exact facial regions showing blending boundaries, skin warping, texture inconsistencies, and generative artifacts.

Video Temporal Frame Analysis

Examines inter-frame consistency, eye-blink frequency, micro-expressions, and audio-lip synchronization across video streams.

Privacy-First Processing

Media is processed transiently in memory with zero permanent facial data retention or third-party cloud exposure.

Tech Stack

PyTorch OpenCV Next.js Python Node.js TailwindCSS TorchVision

FarmOptima

AI-Powered Crop Selling & Mandi Market Optimization

FarmOptima revolutionizes agricultural commerce for farmers by replacing guesswork with economic data science. Rather than chasing headline mandi prices that may be negated by distant transport fees, FarmOptima calculates the "Expected Net Realization" per quintal, considering vehicle rental fees, toll charges, local APMC mandi cess, loading costs, and perishable spoilage risks.

Expected Net Realization Engine

Calculates true take-home profit by deducting logistics, toll costs, loading charges, and mandi commissions from rates.

Multi-Mandi Price Comparison

Aggregates and compares real-time agricultural commodity prices across nearby regional and interstate mandis.

Perishable Spoilage Risk Modeling

Evaluates transit duration against crop shelf-life to prevent transit quality degradation and distress selling.

Multilingual Farmer UI

Clean, high-contrast interface designed for mobile accessibility and regional language support.

MorrowMesh

When the network fails, the people become the network.

MorrowMesh is an offline-first disaster coordination and decentralized communications platform built on the premise that resilient emergency networks can be constructed from everyday civilian hardware. When cellular towers collapse during hurricanes or earthquakes, MorrowMesh enables peer-to-peer relaying of SOS beacons, medical supply requests, and shelter coordinates across localized ad-hoc wireless bridges.

Offline-First Mesh Protocol

Operates completely independent of active cellular data or internet connectivity using ad-hoc localized networks.

Peer-to-Peer SOS Relay

Messages hop across nearby devices until reaching an internet-connected gateway or official rescue headquarters.

Decentralized Incident Board

Local crisis bulletin board for food distribution points, medical supply coordination, and family status checks.

Low-Power Wireless Optimization

Engineered for minimal battery consumption and standard mobile Wi-Fi Direct and Bluetooth hardware.
