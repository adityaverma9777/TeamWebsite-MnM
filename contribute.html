StreetIQ

The AI-Powered Road Intelligence & Navigation Platform

StreetIQ is a next-generation navigation platform that tells you what is actually on the road. It provides real-time turn-by-turn navigation overlaid with live crowdsourced hazard data potholes, waterlogging, cracks, and debris detected entirely on-device using a custom-trained YOLOv8 model running in the browser at 15fps via TensorFlow.js. Video feeds are never sent to a server. Only GPS coordinates and hazard metadata are transmitted, preserving absolute privacy.

Custom-Trained YOLOv8 Model

Hand-trained on thousands of real Indian road images to detect cracks, potholes, waterlogging, and debris across varied lighting and traffic conditions.

On-Device AI — Zero Video Uploads

All inference runs locally in your browser. Nothing leaves your device except GPS coordinates when a hazard is confirmed.

Real-Time Hazard Broadcasting

The moment a hazard is detected, WebSockets instantly broadcast it to every other driver in the area via the Supabase Realtime backend.

Full Navigation Stack

Smooth turn-by-turn navigation powered by OSRM with custom traffic multipliers and Photon geocoding — no third-party map API required.

Tech Stack

React + Vite TensorFlow.js YOLOv8 Node.js + TypeScript OSRM Supabase WebSockets React-Leaflet

Want to contribute to StreetIQ?

  1. Sign up on mnmworks.xyz to create your MnM member account.
  2. Fork the StreetIQ repository on GitHub.

Kavach

Digital Safety & Scam Protection for Senior Citizens · वरिष्ठ नागरिकों के लिए डिजिटल सुरक्षा

Kavach ("shield" in Hindi) is a privacy-first Android app safeguarding senior citizens from digital financial fraud — OTP scams, fake KYC / account-blocked SMS, extortion threats, lottery baits, and malicious APKs distributed via WhatsApp. Operating 100% offline with zero telemetry, Kavach scores threats locally, reads out warnings aloud in plain English or Hindi using on-device text-to-speech, and instantly alerts a trusted family member when high-risk activity is detected.

Automatic SMS Interception

On-device BroadcastReceiver scans incoming SMS in real time, detecting phishing links, banking extortion, urgency patterns, and OTP solicitations with zero cloud transmission.

WhatsApp & Clipboard Scanner

One-tap verification for suspicious forwarded WhatsApp and Telegram messages with bilingual risk assessment (LOW, SUSPICIOUS, HIGH) and audio readout in English and Hindi.

Pre-Install APK Analysis

Deep static manifest analysis inspecting dangerous permissions (READ_SMS, accessibility services, device admin) to flag and block banking trojans before installation.

Trusted Contact & Emergency SOS

Dispatches privacy-safe SMS alerts to a designated family member on high-risk detections, with one-tap dialing for National Cybercrime Helpline 1930 and Chakshu reporting.

Tech Stack

Android SDK (minSdk 26) Kotlin WebView Bridge BroadcastReceiver PackageManager Static Analysis TextToSpeech (en-IN / hi-IN) SQLite Threat DB Firebase Phone Auth
