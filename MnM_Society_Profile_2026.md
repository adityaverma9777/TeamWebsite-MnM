### 1. Overview
Makers Need More (MnM) is a student-led, non-profit organisation focused on making learning, collaboration, and meaningful technology work easier to access. Its stated mission is to empower students globally without barriers by building a student network centred on privacy, learning, innovation, and growth.

MnM presents itself as a community where students can learn by doing, experiment, work on real-world problems, collaborate with peers and mentors, participate in hackathons, contribute to open-source projects, and develop solutions that can move beyond prototypes.

The organisation's approach is built around the belief that the best ideas emerge when students have the freedom, support, and environment to experiment, fail, and grow.

### 2. Mission and Guiding Principles
MnM's mission is to build a student network focused on privacy and learning, while making opportunities for innovation and growth accessible without unnecessary barriers.

The prospectus highlights three guiding principles:

#### 2.1 Privacy First
Participants' data belongs to them. MnM states that it aims to build a safe and secure space for every participant.

#### 2.2 100% Free
The organisation describes its learning environment as free, with no paywalls and no hidden charges. Its stated position is that knowledge should be accessible to everyone.

#### 2.3 Healthy Innovation
MnM aims to maintain a supportive environment where constructive feedback, experimentation, and growth are encouraged.

### 3. Vision and Long-Term Roadmap
MnM's vision is to expand its impact, nurture talent, and democratise access to quality technology education across the globe.

The prospectus identifies three long-term initiatives:

#### 3.1 Global Chapters
MnM plans to expand its student network to universities worldwide by creating local hubs for open-source innovation. Each chapter is intended to operate independently while contributing to a shared knowledge base that benefits the wider community.

#### 3.2 Incubation Program
The proposed incubation programme is intended to provide resources, mentorship, and infrastructure that help student projects evolve into real-world solutions. The support is described as covering the journey from prototype to production.

#### 3.3 Open Learning Hub
MnM envisions a decentralised platform for sharing knowledge, tutorials, and codebase breakdowns curated by contributors. The stated goal is to make community-driven learning accessible to everyone.

### 13. Short Organisational Description
Makers Need More (MnM) is a student-led, non-profit organisation working to make technology education, practical learning, and meaningful opportunities more accessible to students. Built around the principle of learning by doing, MnM brings students together through hackathons, open-source contributions, technical projects, internships, campus outreach, and community-driven initiatives.

The organisation works across frontend development, backend engineering, AI infrastructure, and full-stack solutions. It also supports underprivileged businesses through free websites and digital infrastructure, and helps promising hackathon projects move towards production-ready solutions.

With a vision to build a global student network, MnM aims to nurture talent, encourage collaboration, and democratise access to quality technology education.
